E-Invoice Deadline Checker

Am I affected by the German e-invoice rules?

Answer a few questions. You get your deadlines for receiving and sending e-invoices, what to do before then, and a calendar reminder.

--b2b Do you send invoices to other businesses in Germany?

You and your client are both established in Germany (seat, management or a permanent establishment). Invoices to consumers or to clients abroad do not count.

--kleinunternehmer Are you a Kleinunternehmer under § 19 UStG?

You charge no VAT on your invoices because your turnover is below the small business limits.

--exempt Are all of your invoices to businesses small or VAT exempt?

Every single one is up to €250 gross, a ticket (Fahrausweis), or a VAT-exempt supply under § 4 Nr. 8 to 29 UStG, for example financial services or renting out property.

--turnover-2026 Is your total turnover in 2026 above €800,000?

Your whole turnover for the year (Gesamtumsatz, § 19 Abs. 2 UStG), not only B2B sales. The year 2026 decides whether you start sending in 2027 or in 2028.
